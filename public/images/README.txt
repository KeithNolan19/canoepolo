PHOTOS FOR THE WEBSITE
======================
Put photos in this folder with these exact names and they appear automatically:

  hero.jpg   Big photo at the top of the homepage. Landscape, at least 1920 x 800 pixels.
             Action shots work best (a player shooting at goal, a tackle, a team line-up).
  shop.jpg   Photo on the Shop page (optional).

Photo credit (optional): create a text file with the same name, e.g. hero.txt,
containing one line such as:  Photo: Jane Murphy, Irish Open 2025

Only use photos you took yourself or have permission to use, and credit the photographer.
Tip: keep each photo under ~400 KB (export as JPG, quality 75-80) so the site loads fast.
